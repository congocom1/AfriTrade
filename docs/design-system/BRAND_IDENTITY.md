# Brand Identity

## AfriTrade Brand System

### Brand Overview
AfriTrade is a pan-African digital marketplace connecting sellers and buyers across Africa. The brand represents inclusivity, opportunity, and economic empowerment for African entrepreneurs and consumers.

### Brand Monogram
- **Design**: Geometric pattern inspired by traditional African textiles
- **Colors**: Primary brand colors derived from African flag palettes
- **Usage**: Logo mark for digital presence, stationery, and merchandise

### Voice & Tone
**AfriTrade Voice Characteristics:**
- **Confident**: Empowering and professional
- **Inclusive**: Welcoming to all African voices
- **Accessible**: Clear and straightforward communication
- **Hopeful**: Focused on opportunity and growth
- **Practical**: Solution-oriented and results-driven

**Writing Guidelines:**
- Use active voice and direct language
- Avoid jargon when simple words suffice
- Celebrate African achievements and community
- Emphasize empowerment and opportunity
- Maintain cultural sensitivity and authenticity

### Motion Guidelines
**Brand Animation Philosophy:**
- **Purpose-driven**: Motion serves user experience
- **Subtle**: Enhances without overwhelming
- **Consistent**: Predictable across all interactions
- **Natural**: Follows real-world physics

**Animation Types:**
- **Micro-interactions**: 150ms - 250ms duration
- **Transitions**: 300ms - 500ms duration
- **Page changes**: 500ms - 800ms duration

### "No Map" Rule
**AfriTrade Brand Promise:**
- **Simplicity First**: No complex maps or unnecessary complexity
- **Clear Navigation**: Intuitive user journeys
- **Direct Access**: Fast path to what matters most
- **Minimal Distractions**: Focus on core marketplace activities

**Application:**
- Streamlined checkout processes
- Simplified search and discovery
- Clear call-to-action pathways
- Reduced cognitive load

## Visual Standards

### Color Psychology
AfriTrade uses color to evoke:
- **Trust & Reliability**: Blue variants
- **Growth & Prosperity**: Green variants  
- **Energy & Success**: Orange/gold variants
- **Community & Connection**: Red/variant colors

### Typography Choices
**Primary Font Stack:**
- **Headings**: Inter Bold/Medium for impact
- **Body Text**: Inter Regular for readability
- **Data/Numbers**: JetBrains Mono for clarity

**Font Usage Rules:**
- **Headlines**: Inter Bold, max 2 levels deep
- **Body**: Inter Regular, 16px baseline
- **Labels**: Inter Medium, 14px for clarity
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono, 12px for precision

### Shape Language
**Design Philosophy:**
- **Curved Shapes**: Friendly, approachable feel
- **Geometric Forms**: Modern, professional aesthetic
- **Organic Elements**: Cultural connection and authenticity

**Shape Applications:**
- **Buttons**: Subtle curve for friendly interaction
- **Cards**: Rounded corners for approachability
- **Icons**: Geometric precision for clarity

## Brand Assets

### Logo System
**Components:**
- **Primary Logo**: Full mark with text
- **Icon Mark**: Symbol-only version
- **Monogram**: Simplified geometric pattern
- **Favicon**: Optimized for all devices

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Maintain minimum clear space (1x logo height)
- No stretching or distortion
- Clear space rules on all backgrounds
- Never alter color or shape

### Imagery Guidelines
**Photography Style:**
- **Authentic**: Real African people, places, and products
- **Diverse**: Represent full spectrum of African marketplace
- **High Quality**: Professional photography with natural lighting
- **Contextual**: Images show products in use

**Graphic Elements:**
- Cultural patterns used for visual interest
- African textures and materials
- Geometric overlays for structure
- No generic stock imagery
